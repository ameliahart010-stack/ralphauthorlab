Our story

The story behind Ralph Author Lab

Ralph Author Lab was born from a frustration: watching brilliant books disappear into the noise, not because they lacked substance, but because they never received the visibility they deserved.

Writing a great book is an achievement. Getting it into the hands of the right readers is another challenge entirely, and authors shouldn't have to figure that out alone.

We bring together creative writing, ghostwriting, publishing support and strategic book marketing, giving independent authors the direction and launch support of a major publishing house while keeping their voice their own.

FAQ

Good questions. Clear answers.

My book is already published. Is it too late?

Not at all. Many strong results come from relaunching live books. We start with a free listing audit.

How fast will I see results?

Listing changes can lift conversions within weeks. Reviews and ads usually build over the first 30 to 90 days.

Do you guarantee bestseller status?

No. Nobody honest can. We guarantee a clear plan, careful execution and transparent reporting.

Are your reviews genuine?

Yes. We recruit real readers for an ARC team and never pay for or fake reviews.

What genres do you work with?

Fiction and nonfiction: thrillers, romance, fantasy, YA, memoir, Christian, business and self-help.

Will ghostwritten books still sound like me?

Yes. We study your voice and writing samples first, and you approve every chapter.

Who owns the rights to my book?

You do. Full rights stay with the author.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, ask us on the strategy call. Payment is by bank transfer in GBP, USD or EUR.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to the platform; our fee covers setup, management and reporting.

Is my project confidential?

Yes. Your manuscript and plans stay private.

How do we communicate?

WhatsApp and email, with weekly updates and calls when needed.
